E5 and E6 also failed the holdout comparison against SPY. Raw Ridge/HGB returns are large, but their validation losses and daily tail events make them unsuitable for promotion without additional independent validation and a clearer risk-control case.

## Decision

**No candidate is promoted.** Keep B0 frozen and F75 shadow-only. Treat E4c cap-1x as the conservative reference candidate for any follow-up analysis, not as a winner: it reduces risk but does not beat SPY over the holdout. Any future candidate must be compared against the frozen controls with consistent split-specific drawdown calculations, terminal balance, annual returns, tail loss, turnover/cost sensitivity, and bear-market/recovery behavior.
