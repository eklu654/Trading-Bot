## Next decision
Stop spending runs on small threshold variations in the current structural matrix. It does not beat B0's terminal wealth on actual TQQQ, and its event sample is too small to validate a classifier. Preserve the canonical -4.5% QQQ daily shock / +10% from post-shock running-low recovery as the control. The most valuable next work is a pre-registered, walk-forward test of broader feature families with a hard out-of-sample split and a small number of independent historical events, plus fixing the mismatched three-layer validation pipeline before its output is used. Keep the target explicit: improve end balance without sacrificing the 2019/COVID rebound and test historical 1987, 2000–02, 2008, and 2022 cases; do not treat synthetic 3x QQQ as real TQQQ.
